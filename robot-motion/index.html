Navigation Controls Guide

  • Aim & Select: Move your hand cursor over any button or card. Hold steady for the dwell timer to select automatically.
  • Cross Hands in 'X': Cross your hands or forearms in an 'X' shape in front of you to return to previous menu or dismiss a dialog.
  • Browse Cards: Swipe left hand to the left for previous card, or right hand to the right for next card. In single-hand mode, swipe that hand left or right. Or use ← / → arrow keys.
  • Keyboard Select: Press Enter or Space on any card or button.
  • Keyboard Back: Press Esc anytime to go back.
  • Posture: Standing mode is active with full-body motion tracking.
  • Toggle Selecting Hand: Press H to cycle selecting hand between Both Hands, Right, and Left.
